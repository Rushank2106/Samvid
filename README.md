# Samvida

## AI-Powered Government Scheme Eligibility and Benefit Discovery

> **From Eligibility to Entitlement**

Samvida is an AI-powered web platform designed to help citizens discover, understand, prepare for, and access government schemes that they may be eligible for.

India has numerous Central and State government schemes, but citizens often face difficulties in identifying relevant schemes, understanding eligibility criteria, knowing the required documents, and finding the correct official application portal. Samvida aims to simplify this journey by providing personalized scheme discovery, eligibility-based recommendations, document readiness guidance, an AI assistant, multilingual interaction, and verified official links.

## Problem Statement

India has numerous government welfare and benefit schemes for students, farmers, workers, entrepreneurs, families, senior citizens, and other sections of society.

However, citizens often struggle to identify schemes relevant to their situation, understand eligibility criteria, know the benefits they can receive, identify required documents, determine which documents are missing, and find the correct official application portal. Language and accessibility barriers can further make this information difficult to access.

This creates a gap between **government benefits being available and citizens actually accessing them**.

---

## Our Solution

Samvida creates a personalized journey:

**Discover → Understand → Check Eligibility → Prepare Documents → Apply → Track**

Users provide basic information such as age, location, occupation, income, employment type, family details, and requirements.

Based on this information, Samvida identifies **potentially relevant government schemes** and provides structured information about their benefits, eligibility criteria, required documents, application process, and official application links.

Samvida goes beyond basic scheme discovery by helping users understand their document readiness and interact with an AI-powered assistant for personalized guidance.

## Key Features

### 1. Personalized Scheme Discovery

Users enter basic information such as:

- Age
- State and district
- Occupation
- Employment type
- Income range
- Family details
- Requirements or interests

Samvida uses this information to identify potentially relevant government schemes.

### 2. Eligibility-Based Recommendations

The platform evaluates the user's profile against available eligibility information and presents potentially relevant schemes.

Users can also filter schemes based on:

- Central Government Schemes
- State Government Schemes

### 3. Detailed Scheme Information

Users can explore:

- Scheme benefits
- Eligibility criteria
- Required documents
- Application process
- Official source
- Official application portal

### 4. Document Readiness Checker

Samvida helps users understand the documents required for a particular scheme.

It can highlight:

- Available documents
- Missing documents
- Required document information
- Relevant document resources or guidance

### 5. Samvida AI Assistant

The Samvida Assistant provides a conversational interface for scheme-related queries.

Users can:

- Ask questions about government schemes
- Understand eligibility and benefits
- Access relevant document links
- Get application guidance
- Discover additional schemes that may match their eligibility

### 6. Multilingual Interaction

Samvida is designed to improve accessibility by supporting interaction in multiple Indian languages.

Users can switch languages while using the platform and interacting with the assistant.

### 7. Voice-Based Interaction

Voice interaction provides an additional accessibility option, allowing users to interact with Samvida through voice-based input.

### 8. Verified Official Links

Samvida provides relevant official government sources and application portals so users can continue their application through the appropriate official channel.

### 9. Scheme Comparison

Users can compare relevant schemes and understand differences in their benefits, eligibility requirements, and documents.

### 10. Personalized Benefit Journey

Samvida focuses on the complete journey:

**Eligibility → Understanding → Document Preparation → Application**

This forms the core concept of Samvida:

> **From Eligibility to Entitlement**
